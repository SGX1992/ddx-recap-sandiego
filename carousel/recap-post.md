# DDX'26 San Diego — LinkedIn recap post (DDX page)

That's a wrap on DDX'26 San Diego.

One day, three stages, 29 speakers and 400+ design and product leaders at UC San Diego Park & Market, all on one question: if AI can produce "good enough" design on demand, what is the job of a design leader now?

Five things the room agreed on:

– Good is free now. Great is the job.
– Research stops being a project and becomes a pulse.
– Stop designing screens. Start designing decisions.
– The human stays irrational, and irreplaceable.
– Context is the asset. The experience is the business.
